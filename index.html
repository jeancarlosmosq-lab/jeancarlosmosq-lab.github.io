Tranque: Dominoes Scorekeeper

A scorekeeper for Dominican-style partnership dominó — for the table, in English and Español.

  • Count from photo: photograph the tiles left in hand and it counts the pips
  • Capicúa, tranque, pollona — scored the way tables actually play
  • Player profiles and full game history
  • Music search and playback without leaving the app
